The Opportunity

Restaurants deserve better than chaos

Owners told us the same story everywhere: menus stuck in PDFs, stock tracked in notebooks, tasks scattered across WhatsApp, money buried in Excel. Nothing talks to anything else — and enterprise software feels cold, expensive, and overbuilt for a dhaba or a growing chain.

The pain today

  • Reprinted menus every time a price changes
  • Last-minute market runs because stock wasn't tracked
  • Opening checklists lost in group chats
  • Table orders scribbled on paper, re-entered later
  • Finance split across outlets with no single view
  • Staff given full access — or locked out entirely

The RestoHub answer

  • One hub for every outlet you operate
  • Live QR menus guests scan — no app download
  • Ingredient stock with low-level alerts
  • Prep, opening & closing checklists with accountability
  • Visual table board with in-house menu orders
  • Role-based team access — invite only, no random signups
